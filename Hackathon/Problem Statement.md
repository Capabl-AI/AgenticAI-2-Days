# Problem Statement: Build Your Digital Twin with Tools and RAG

Imagine having a smart AI version of yourself that talks like you, shares your skills from your resume, and helps you in everyday tasks. Your challenge is to create this "digital twin" using LangChain, where you can choose any large language model (LLM) and embedding model you prefer.

Use your own resume, personal bio, or a custom personality you imagine to build the knowledge base. The digital agent should be able to have natural conversations, answer questions about your background, and assist with daily needs.

**Requirements:**
- Your agent must include at least **two useful tools** (such as a calculator, calendar, or web search) to interact with the world and perform tasks.
- Implement **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** to access and provide accurate information from your knowledge base during conversations.

**Judging Criteria:**
- How helpful the personal agent is in daily life tasks and questions.
- How well the agent represents and impresses job recruiters with your skills and personality.

Build a practical and smart agent that feels like a digital version of you, enhanced with useful tools and the ability to recall your personal information effectively. This challenge pushes you to combine creativity, AI reasoning, and tool integration to create a truly personal AI companion.
